Please follow these 5 quick steps to get started as a volunteer:

  1. Join our next CommuniTEA call : you'll find our next online gathering on our community calendar. It takes places every last Wednesday of the month from 6-7 PM (CEST time). 

  2. Create an Open Food Facts user account here (if you haven't got one already!). 

  3. Get familiar with this wiki page dedicated to Volunteer activity and read the specific page on the team that interests you the most.  

  4. Reach out to one of the team referents on Slack or by email (see below) 

  5. Finally, please read through our community’s Code of Conduct. 

Monthly meet-ups  

Please note that we host a CommuniTEA meet-up once a month, to welcome new volunteers and exchange together with the other members of our community. 

After the first half hour of connecting, it’s often followed by a 30-min tutorial on various topics, geared towards beginners (some previous topics: “New website features”, “Data quality tools”, “How to promote food transparency?”).  

You'll find this event on our community calendar. 

Reach out to a team referent 

You can do it on our Slack (a place where most of the community conversations take place) : https://slack.openfoodfacts.org/ or by sending an email to contact@openfoodfacts.org (with the subject: ex. "Team Tech")
